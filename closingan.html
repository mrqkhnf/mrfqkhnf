Closing Tomoro

Sistem kalkulator closing stock bahan Tomoro Coffee

✓ Data otomatis tersimpan walaupun refresh
✓ Tampilan mobile friendly tanpa geser layar

Hasil Perhitungan

Catatan Perhitungan

  • Powder otomatis dikurangi berat toples 200gr.
  • Syrup otomatis dikurangi berat pump 45gr.
  • Premade otomatis dikurangi botol squeeze 60gr.
  • Rumus Powder = (timbangan - 200) ÷ gross weight.
  • Rumus Syrup = (timbangan - 45) ÷ gross weight.
  • Rumus Premade = (timbangan - 60) ÷ 2 lalu ditambahkan ke hasil powder yang sama.
  • Data otomatis tersimpan walaupun halaman ter-refresh.
